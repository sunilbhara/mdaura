# The reader

The reader is built for long documents. It keeps your place, lets you search, and gets out of the way when you want to just read.

## Outline

Every heading from level 1 to level 3 appears in the outline on the left. The section you are reading is marked, and the percentage at the top shows how far through the document you are.

- Select a heading to jump to it.
- Press <kbd>[</kbd> to show or hide the outline.
- On small screens the outline opens over the page and closes after you pick a heading.

## Search

Press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> (or <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> on a Mac) to search. Results show the section each match belongs to. Press <kbd>Enter</kbd> to jump to the first result, or select any result in the list.

Search looks at the text of the document, including code blocks and table cells.

## Focus mode

Press <kbd>.</kbd> or select the eye icon to enter focus mode. The outline closes and the toolbar fades out until you move the pointer to the top of the window. Press <kbd>.</kbd> again to leave.

## Text size

Use the control in the bottom-right corner, or press <kbd>+</kbd> and <kbd>-</kbd>. Select the percentage to go back to 100%. The size applies to the document only, not to the toolbar.

## Linking to a section

Hover over a heading and select the link icon next to it. The address of that section is copied to your clipboard and the page scrolls to it.

## Reading time

The bottom of the outline shows the word count and an estimated reading time, based on 220 words a minute.
