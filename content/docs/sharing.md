# Sharing

Share links are **coming soon**. When they arrive, you will be able to send a link that opens your document in MDAura for anyone, with the outline, search and diagrams intact. The person you send it to will not need an account.

Selecting **Share** in the reader today opens a short note about this, with export options.

## Share a document today

Export a copy and send the file:

| You want to send | Export as | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Something people can edit or comment on | Word | Diagrams are included as images. |
| A fixed copy that looks the same everywhere | PDF | Use your browser's print dialog and choose **Save as PDF**. |
| The source, for someone who uses Markdown | Markdown | The original file, unchanged. |
| A slide deck | PowerPoint | Marp files only. |

See [Exporting](/docs/exporting) for details on each format.

## What share links will do

- Open the document in the same reader you use, with no **Share** button.
- Keep embedded images, so the recipient sees everything you see.
- Stay out of search engines.
- Stop working when you revoke them or when they expire, and delete the stored copy at the same time.

Progress is tracked in the [release notes](/releases).
